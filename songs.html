Recommend a song

The list has stayed at twenty for a year, so a new track has to push one out. If you have a candidate, send it — the form is plain HTML and opens your own mail program.

Nothing is stored on a server: the message leaves through your mail client.

The rule

  • A song enters only if it survives a year.
  • One song per singer, unless the singer is Gareth.T.
  • No skipping the first thirty seconds.
  • The list ends softly, always.
